# Roadmap

What we're working on next. It has no dates and the order isn't fixed: what gets built first depends on what teams ask for. The full version, with the principles behind it, is on the blog: [Estimay roadmap](https://estimay.io/blog/estimay-roadmap).

Want something moved up, or is something missing? [Open a feature request](https://github.com/grynyk/estimay-public/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) or give 👍 to an existing one.

## Bring stories in, get estimates out

- **Copy to clipboard:** copy a session's results straight into a spreadsheet or a ticket.
- **More trackers:** GitLab Issues and ClickUp, with estimates written back as Jira, Linear, Trello, Asana and GitHub do today.

## Estimate across time zones

- **Reminders** that a story is waiting for your vote in an async room.

## Keep and learn from your estimates

- **Session summary:** one page with every story, its votes, the agreed estimate and the total.
- **Optional sign-in** with email, Google or GitHub, to keep your rooms on every device. Joining will never need an account.
- **Optional history:** a signed-in host can save a room. Rooms that aren't saved are still deleted after 7 days (30 for async).
- **Team insights:** how often the team reaches consensus, which stories split the votes most, and how estimates add up across sessions.

## Fit into your team's tools

- **Public API:** create rooms and read results from your own scripts and bots, with personal access tokens and an OpenAPI description.
- **Webhooks** when an estimate is set or a room ends, for Zapier, n8n, Make or your own service.
- **Chat notifications:** post a session summary to a Slack or Microsoft Teams channel.

## For every team, everywhere

- **In your language:** the site and the room in German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Ukrainian, Turkish, Japanese and Polish.
- **For larger organisations:** single sign-on, an audit log and admin controls.

## Planning poker for AI-assisted teams

- **Story notes:** the goal, scope, constraints and open questions the team agreed on, kept next to the estimate and in the CSV.
- **Questions from the ? card:** whoever plays ? can type the question behind it, listed under the story after the reveal.
- **Copy as an agent-ready brief:** a story, its estimate and its notes as Markdown, ready for a ticket or an agent's prompt.
- **Actuals and calibration:** import what each story really used (tokens, passes, review time) and see it next to the estimate.

## What stays the same

- Joining stays instant: a link and a name, no account.
- Anything that stores more, or shares data with another service, is opt-in, and the Privacy Policy is updated first.
- An AI never votes, and never shows a suggested estimate before the team's cards are revealed.
